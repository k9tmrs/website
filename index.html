How It Works

Simple, Smartphone-Powered Timing

K9 TMRS makes timing dog sports events simple and accessible. Using your own Android or iOS smartphone, you can display timing information directly on your device. Our intuitive setup process ensures you’re ready to go in minutes.

  1. 1

    Download the K9 TMRS app

    Install the K9 TMRS app on your smartphone to unlock powerful, portable timing capabilities. Android is available now in early access, and iOS is available on request via TestFlight.

  2. 2

    Connect your device to the timing system

    Pair your smartphone with the K9 TMRS timing hardware so you can control runs and monitor results in real time.

  3. 3

    Start timing your event and view results in real time

    Run your classes, capture accurate times, and see results instantly on your device and for your show processor.

Get started in three steps — download the app, connect to the timing system, and start timing your runs.

Hoops

Made-to-Order Hoops

Alongside our timing system, we supply Hoopers hoops made to order. Each hoop is built individually for your club or event to a consistent, competition-ready standard.

Designed for dogs

Every hoop is made to order and built to a consistent, competition-ready standard — just let us know how many you need and we’ll take care of the rest.

  • Baseless design with a detachable, locking foot — stable in use, yet quick to break down for transport and storage.
  • High-visibility blue — research into canine colour vision shows dogs are dichromats who see blue clearly while reds and greens appear muted and grey, so a blue hoop stands out and is easier for them to sight and line up (Neitz, Geist & Jacobs, 1989).
  • Lightweight for easy carrying, transport, and single-handed course setup.
  • Soft construction to help prevent injury if a dog clips a hoop mid-run.
